Welcome to SMB2 RUNZ

Q and A

Q - Who made this website?
A - Epick


Q - How do I submit a run?
A - You need a Discord account. Contact @epickfr on Discord.


Q - Is TAS allowed?
A - Yes. TAS runs are allowed, but they must be submitted under the TAS category.


Q - Do you support full-game runs?
A - No. SMB2 Runz only supports level runs.
Example: Practice Mode runs.


Q - Why not use Speedrun.com?
A - Speedrun.com does not support SMB2 level leaderboards.
